# Dugout Brand Strategy

_Established 2026-08-01. Reference this before writing copy, designing UI, or making product decisions that affect the user experience._

---

## Brand Foundation

**Mission**: Handle the chaos. Protect the moments.

**Vision**: A world where every youth sports family feels fully connected, organized, and part of something bigger — regardless of sport, town, or team.

**Brand Promise**: Dugout handles the logistics so families can focus on the moments.

**Positioning**: The only youth sports coordination app built for real parents, not administrators.

**Unique POV**: Youth sports coordination isn't a software problem — it's a community problem. Tools that treat it like a workflow miss what actually matters.

**Core Values**:
1. **Community over coordination** — The tool exists to serve relationships, not replace them
2. **Simplicity as respect** — Respecting users' time means building things simply
3. **Warmth in every detail** — Every touchpoint should feel human, not transactional
4. **Built for the field** — Designed for real life: outdoors, distracted, one-handed, between innings
5. **Joy in the small stuff** — Even a payment reminder can feel like a high-five

**Customer Transformation**:

| Before | After |
|--------|-------|
| Scattered across 5+ apps | Everything in one place |
| Missing messages and updates | Always in the loop |
| Stressed about logistics | Present at the game |
| Part of a group chat | Part of a team |

## Brand Story

Every baseball season starts the same way: a parking lot, folding chairs, and a group chat with 30 people nobody asked permission to add.

Then come the questions. *"What time is practice?" "Wait, is that home or away?" "Did anyone else not get the email?"*

Youth sports should be about kids. It should be about the crack of the bat, the smell of cut grass, sunscreen and Gatorade and cleats in the trunk. It should be about memories.

Instead, it became a coordination nightmare. And parents became project managers.

Dugout was built to change that. Not by adding another app to the pile. By replacing all of them.

When the logistics disappear into the background, the moments move to the foreground.

**Messaging Pillars**:
1. **Everything in one place** — No more switching apps or missing threads
2. **Built for this community** — Designed for parents on bleachers, not admins at desks
3. **Simple by design** — If it takes more than 10 seconds, we built it wrong
4. **For the whole team** — Coaches, parents, players — everyone's included
5. **Season after season** — A tool that becomes part of how your team works

## Brand Personality

**Archetype**: The Supportive Coach meets The Organized Team Parent

**Tone**: Warm. Direct. Confident without being cocky. Celebratory without being over the top.

**Voice Rules**:
- Short sentences. Active voice.
- Speaks like a coach giving a pregame speech, not a lawyer writing terms of service
- Celebrates small wins
- Never patronizing — speaks to parents as capable, busy adults
- Uses sports energy without sports clichés

**Voice Examples**:

| Generic | Dugout Voice |
|---------|-------------|
| "An error occurred. Please try again." | "Something didn't work. Give it another shot." |
| "No announcements found." | "Nothing posted yet. First announcement?" |
| "Transaction processed successfully." | "Payment logged. ✓" |
| "Welcome to the Dugout Platform." | "You're in the dugout." |

## Visual Direction

**Design Language**: _Stadium at sunset_ — deep sky going dark, amber stadium lights coming on, fresh-cut grass. High contrast, warm light, dark depth.

**Color Philosophy**:
- **Ember Orange (#FF5C1A)** — Energy, action, warmth. Stadium lights and momentum. Use for CTAs, badges, focal points.
- **Field Green (#2ECC71)** — Growth, success, completion. Use for positive states.
- **Pitch (dark palette)** — Focus and depth. Primary surface.
- **White** — Clarity and precision. Names, scores, essentials.

**Typography**:
- **Barlow Condensed** (display) — Athletic energy, headlines, CTAs
- **DM Sans** (body) — Clean, friendly, readable
- Explore Barlow Condensed ExtraBold for hero/celebration moments

**Illustration Style**:
- Warm, simple SVG illustrations (continue current approach)
- Diverse, relatable characters
- Motion and energy in posture
- Baseball elements as background texture, not foreground decoration

**Motion Principles**:
- Transitions: slide up + fade in — never jarring
- Loading: pulse, not spin (calmer, more confident)
- Success: subtle scale pop + confetti/emoji burst for big completions
- Button presses: slight scale-down on tap, scale-up on release

## Copy Standards

**Empty States**:

| Location | Copy |
|----------|------|
| Schedule (coach) | "No games on the board yet. Add your first event and let the season begin." |
| Schedule (player) | "Nothing scheduled yet. Your coach is probably working on it — check back soon." |
| Announcements (coach) | "First announcement? Your team is listening." |
| Announcements (player) | "Nothing from Coach yet. Stay tuned." |
| Payments (player) | "You're all clear. No payments due right now." |
| Roster (empty) | "Your dugout is empty. Share the invite code and build your crew." |

**Success Messages**:

| Action | Copy |
|--------|------|
| Team created | "Your dugout is ready. Time to build your crew." |
| RSVP submitted | "You're in. See you at the field." |
| Announcement posted | "Your team just got the news." |
| Payment logged | "Payment logged. ✓" |
| Invite copied | "Link copied — send it to your crew." |

**Error Messages**:

| Error | Copy |
|-------|------|
| Generic | "Something didn't work. Give it another shot." |
| Network | "Can't reach the dugout right now. Check your connection." |
| Auth failure | "That login didn't work. Try again?" |
| Permission | "Only coaches can do this." |

**Push Notifications** (when added):
- Event tomorrow: "Game day tomorrow. [Team] at [Time]. You in?"
- Payment due: "[Team]: $[Amount] due [Date]."
- New announcement: "[Coach] posted: [First 60 chars]..."

**Headlines / Marketing**:
- "Your team. All in one place."
- "One app. Every season."
- "Stop managing. Start coaching."
- "Handle the logistics. Protect the moments."

**CTAs**:
- "Build Your Roster" (invite sheet)
- "Share with Your Crew" (join code label)
- "See You at the Field" (RSVP confirmation)
- "Get in the Dugout" (marketing signup)

## Logo System

1. **Primary**: Wordmark "DUGOUT" (Barlow Condensed ExtraBold) + icon — web, marketing
2. **Secondary**: Icon only — app icon, favicon
3. **Tertiary**: D monogram — watermark, backgrounds

**Logo refinement goals**:
- Baseball laces as signature element, not decoration
- Test uppercase vs lowercase "D" for warmth vs. athletic authority
- Wordmark tracked out slightly for legibility at all sizes

## Naming

**Keep Dugout.** It earns its name:
- Baseball-coded without being limiting
- Metaphorically rich — where teams come together between innings
- Short, memorable, conversational: "Did you check Dugout?"

**The Hive** as parent brand has expansion potential:
- Dugout (baseball)
- Pitch (soccer)
- Court (basketball)

Hold this architecture in mind. Don't act on it yet.

## Brand Roadmap

**Phase 1 — Foundation** (pre-launch)
- Rewrite all empty states, error messages, and success toasts with brand voice
- Add contextual loading labels ("Loading your team...")
- Audit every CTA for voice consistency

**Phase 2 — Earn Trust** (MVP launch)
- Marketing landing page with brand story and "Get in the Dugout" CTA
- Push notification and email copy templates
- Begin capturing user stories from early teams

**Phase 3 — Community** (post-traction)
- Referral flow: "Tell your team's team about Dugout"
- Season-end celebration moments in product
- Real user photography for marketing

**Phase 4 — The Hive** (expansion)
- Multi-sport product architecture under The Hive parent brand
- Design system scales across product family
